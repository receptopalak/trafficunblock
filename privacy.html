Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Version 1.0.1

Short version: Traffic Unblock collects anonymous usage data so the levels can be improved. It is tied to a random identifier, never to your name or e-mail, and is never used to track you across other apps or websites. There are no ads and no advertising identifier. You can switch the collection off inside the game at any time.

What is collected and why

DataWhy
Random anonymous id, session id, session number, days since installTo group events of one installation and measure whether players come back
Which level was started, completed or lost; number of moves, attempts, hints and undos; time taken; stars earned; reason for losingTo find the levels that are too hard, too easy or confusing
Device model, operating system and version, language, time zone offset, approximate country, screen size, pixel density, refresh rate, processor, graphics adapter, memoryTo know which devices the game must run well on
Frame rate samplesTo find the devices where the game runs badly
App versionTo compare releases
A per-app device identifier (identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android)To tell installations apart when the saved random id is lost. It is specific to this app, is not the advertising identifier, and resets when you uninstall the game.

What is never collected

Name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, payment information, and the advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID). The game asks for none of these permissions and contains no advertising network.

Approximate country

The country is derived from the IP address of the request by the analytics provider. The IP address itself is not stored by the developer and is not used to identify you.

Who processes the data

Usage data is processed by Mixpanel on servers in the European Union. No other third party receives it, and it is never sold.

Turning it off

In the game: Settings → Usage data. When switched off, nothing is sent from the next launch onwards. The game is fully playable with it off.

Data kept only on your device

Your level progress, stars, best times and preferences are stored on the phone only and are never transmitted. They are deleted when you uninstall the game, or through Settings → Reset progress.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. No data that could identify a person is collected, from children or from anyone else.

Your rights

Because the data is not linked to a person, the developer cannot look you up in it. If you want the events from your installation deleted, write from the address below with the anonymous id shown in the game under Settings → About, and they will be removed.

Third-party components

The game is built with the open source Godot Engine.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. All map data is available under the Open Database License.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published at the same address and the date above is updated.

Asla toplanmayanlar

Ad, e-posta adresi, telefon numarası, posta adresi, kesin konum, rehber, fotoğraflar, kamera, mikrofon, ödeme bilgisi ve reklam kimliği (IDFA / GAID). Oyun bu izinlerin hiçbirini istemez ve içinde reklam ağı yoktur.

Yaklaşık ülke

Ülke bilgisi, isteğin IP adresinden analiz sağlayıcısı tarafından çözülür. IP adresinin kendisi geliştirici tarafından saklanmaz ve seni tanımlamak için kullanılmaz.

Veriyi kim işliyor

Kullanım verisi Mixpanel tarafından Avrupa Birliği'ndeki sunucularda işlenir. Başka hiçbir üçüncü tarafa verilmez ve hiçbir şekilde satılmaz.

Nasıl kapatılır

Oyun içinde: Ayarlar → Kullanım verisi. Kapatıldığında bir sonraki açılıştan itibaren hiçbir şey gönderilmez. Oyun kapalıyken de eksiksiz oynanır.

Yalnızca cihazında kalanlar

Bölüm ilerlemen, yıldızların, en iyi sürelerin ve tercihlerin yalnızca telefonda tutulur ve hiçbir zaman gönderilmez. Uygulamayı silince ya da Ayarlar → İlerlemeyi sıfırla ile birlikte silinir.

Çocuklar

Oyun her yaşa uygundur. Ne çocuklardan ne de başkasından kişiyi tanımlayabilecek bir veri toplanmaz.

Hakların

Veri bir kişiye bağlı olmadığı için geliştirici içinde seni arayamaz. Kendi kurulumuna ait olayların silinmesini istersen, oyunda Ayarlar → Hakkında altında görünen anonim kimlikle aşağıdaki adrese yaz; kayıtlar silinir.
